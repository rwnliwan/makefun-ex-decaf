One-word verdict on an ex: **decaf**.

An ex as decaf coffee — looks exactly like the real thing, zero effect.
The cup is familiar, the ritual is familiar, the kick never arrives.

Entry for [Describe Your Ex in One Word](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7) on make.fun.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single word `decaf` to `/output/entry.txt`. No input,
no network, no dependencies — the word is the whole entry.
